My excerpt covers only the scanning half, and the rest presumably dispatches on the matched action, so a reviewer has to hold the cached-versus-stored position state in their head across all of it.

My recommendation: confirm whether a generator emits this method, and if so, review the grammar and generator version instead of the output. If it is hand-maintained, the refill-and-resync block is the first extract-method candidate, since it is a self-contained sequence of store-back, refill, and reload steps.
